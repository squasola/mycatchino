About Chino


I adopted chino in June of 2025. He is a brown longhaired tabby who only has half of his tail due to an infection he had as a kitten. I named him Chino after the lead vocalist of the band Deftones, since I thought it would be a cute name for a cat. He is very sleepy for his age and spends most of his time lounging on furniture in my room, sometimes in really silly positions. Despite his laid-back nature, he has a feral side to him. He is an absolutely vicious beast, and loves to play rough with both his toys and myself alike. I play music for him all the time, as alot of my life revolves around it. Some of his favorute artists are Radiohead, Aphex Twin, and Sigur Ros. Overall, Chino is a cool cat and an awesome roommate; plus, how could you not like that handsome face?
